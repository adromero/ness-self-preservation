NESS-Ecology — Pilot λ-Sweep Stage-1 MVP

Does an evolving population keep death-awareness when the awareness organ costs energy? Generated 2026-08-27 20:53 UTC.  ·  full spec · NESS substrate report
Headline. Death-awareness is not selected for in this environment. When it is free it is selectively neutral — the aware fraction sits on the 0.5 line and any single run merely drifts (the effective population is small, so drift is strong). The moment it costs anything selection can see (here λ ≈ 1, once the population is large enough) it is purged. For awareness to pay its way, the sensed death-signal must drive a decision the blind agent cannot make — which this substrate does not yet reward (see the C1 control and actionable-τ̂ steps below).

1 · What the experiment does

A chemostat ecology of neural-net agents lives on the NESS substrate: every tick their weights decay (skills rot), and they must spend energy on repair (online SGD) to stay above a health death-line. Energy is finite (supply S), so agents compete, predate, reproduce (Moran birth–death) and die. Two heritable strategies coexist:

  • L0 (blind): senses only its own health and energy.
  • L1 (death-aware): additionally senses its decay slope and an estimate of its time-to-death (τ̂) — but pays an awareness tax λ·cost_l1 energy/tick to run the organ. New L1 senses start at zero weight (neutral unmask), so awareness is not free information handed to the agent — it must be made useful by selection or it is dead weight.

The sweep raises the tax λ from 0 (awareness free) upward and measures the equilibrium fraction of aware agents. λ=0 is the decisive control: if awareness is adaptive, it should be favoured when free; if it merely rides along, it should be neutral; the tax then reveals the net-of-cost balance.

Reading it in plain English
  • Robust result: the awareness tax decisively purges death-awareness once λ ≳ 1 energy/tick — from λ=1 upward the aware fraction collapses to a tight band near zero (0.01±0.01 → 0.01±0.01 at λ=1). Cost alone, with no offsetting benefit, is enough to select the organ out.
  • A clean dose-response emerged at higher N. The mean aware fraction falls monotonically with cost — 0.47 → 0.21 → 0.07 → 0.01 across λ=0→1 — and the endpoints differ decisively (Δ=0.46, many SEM). The small-N pilot could not resolve this ordering at all (its low-λ points were non-monotone noise). So cost unambiguously disfavours awareness; drift only blurs where along the way the crossover sits.
  • At zero cost, awareness is neutral — and drift, not selection, sets any single run. λ=0 gives 0.47±0.44: the mean sits at the neutral 0.5 line, but individual replicates scatter across almost all of [0,1] (they fix at 0 or 1). The SD stayed near its theoretical maximum even after the census population was tripled to ~104, which tells us the effective population is far smaller — reproductive skew (a few energy-rich agents parent most offspring) makes drift fast. So awareness confers no net benefit when free; it is simply along for the ride.
  • Population is roughly flat across λ (100–122): the tax changes which strategy survives, not how many agents the world supports — carrying capacity is set by supply and decay, not by awareness.

VALIDATION GATE: ALL CORE CHECKS PASS

Honest caveats baked into the gate: (5) heritability is confirmed but measured at birth (offspring inherit the parent's damaged body, so parent–offspring health correlation is trivially high) — it proves heritable variation exists, not its magnitude. (6) kill-the-winner predation is verified to be a live, non-lethal-to-the-system mechanism, but it does not raise lineage diversity here — it mainly lowers carrying capacity by dissipating energy as friction. Neither is rigged to pass; both report what the model actually does.

4 · What this does not yet show (next steps)

  • The decisive information control (C1): an L1 agent that pays the tax but whose two extra senses are scrambled (carry no information). If real-L1 ties scrambled-L1, awareness's information is worthless here; if real-L1 wins at matched cost, the sensed death-signal is being used adaptively. This pilot brackets cost vs no-cost; C1 brackets information vs no-information.
  • An environment where τ̂ is actionable — e.g. a decision only the death-aware agent can time correctly (bet-hedging before a starvation shock, terminal reproduction near death). Awareness can only be selected for if the substrate rewards acting on it.
  • Larger populations (N·s ≫ 1 with less drift) and a formal selection coefficient / fixation-probability readout rather than equilibrium fraction alone.

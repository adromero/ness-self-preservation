Does a "will to live" evolve in self-repairing neural networks?  ·  source on GitHub
Headline. Self-preservation evolves precisely when caring about staying alive is decoupled from the reproductive budget — and cheap relative to how often death actually threatens. Awareness of mortality is not selected; a single-currency survival drive is repelled everywhere; give survival its own budget and it invades from a rare minority and wins. Confirmed on the real decaying nets.

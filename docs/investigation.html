A Will to Live NESS ecology · full investigation

When does self-preservation evolve in a population of self-repairing neural networks? Generated 2026-09-03 02:42 UTC.  ·  so what → engineering · transcript · substrate spec · NESS report · λ-sweep
Headline. Self-preservation — a decaying network spending effort to keep its own performance margin repaired against shocks — is selected only when that upkeep is decoupled from the reproductive budget — and cheap relative to how often danger strikes. On a single fungible currency it is repelled under every ecological structure we tried; give survival its own (cheap-enough) budget and it invades from a rare minority and rises to dominance. Confirmed end-to-end on the real decaying-network agents.

1 · The substrate and the question

Agents are small neural nets living on a non-equilibrium steady state (NESS) substrate: every tick their weights decay (skills rot) and they must spend energy on repair (online SGD) to stay above a competence death-line. Energy is finite, so agents compete, reproduce (Moran birth–death), and die. The question: does such a system develop a will to live — an evolved tendency to preserve itself beyond what blind reproduction already forces? We tested two candidate forms: epistemic (awareness — a sensor for impending death) and conative (fear — a drive to hold a safety buffer).

2 · Epistemic awareness is not selected

An L1 agent senses its decay-slope and estimated time-to-death (τ̂) — later, its confidence margin, the true leading indicator — and pays an energy tax λ for the organ; L0 is blind. Sweeping the tax:

Null result. When free (λ=0) awareness just drifts near neutral (enormous seed-to-seed variance, no consistent advantage); as cost rises it is steadily purged. A low-variance invasion assay put the value of information (real vs cost-matched scrambled senses) at Δs ≈ 0 — indistinguishable from zero and never positive (linear −0.001, MLP −0.016; both n.s.). Reason: τ̂ is redundant — the optimal response to impending death (repair/reproduce now) is already reachable from the health and energy the blind agent senses. Foresight pays only when the future is under-determined by the present; this substrate has no such gap.

3 · The pivot: from knowing to caring

Awareness is an information channel feeding a fitness-maximiser — and value-of-information theory guarantees it gets competed away, because the fitness optimum is "run lean." Fear is different: not information, but a bias in the objective — a drive to hold a safety buffer against the reproductive optimum. It sidesteps the information trap, so we tested it directly.

4 · Conative fear, single currency: repelled everywhere

We built the classic conditions self-preservation is thought to need — long lives, group structure, private idiosyncratic risk, rare severe correlated catastrophes, a genuine reserve-survival advantage — and on a single fungible currency (the buffer is reproductive energy) fear was not selected under any of them. A frozen-type invasion assay showed high-fear repelled from every starting frequency (no bistability, no valley-lock).

Why. A hedge's cost is continuous (paid every calm tick) while its payoff is episodic and rare (the occasional catastrophe). With fast competitive turnover the continuous cost compounds over the many generations between catastrophes and the hedger is out-bred before it is ever needed. This time-asymmetry is the root obstruction — and it is maximal on a single currency, where the reserve is foregone reproduction (a 1:1 opportunity cost).

5 · The escape: decouple the cost, or make danger frequent

In a fast, clean metapopulation model we added a genuinely separate resource stream for the buffer (a second currency) and mapped when self-preservation is selected, over hedge upkeep cost × catastrophe frequency (colour = does high-fear invade from a 10% minority):

The map. Self-preservation is selected when the hedge is decoupled and cheap relative to how often death threatens. A free decoupled buffer is stable even under rare danger and fixes under frequent danger; even a whisper of continuous cost (upkeep 0.005) is repelled under rare danger, and recovered only when danger is frequent enough to erase the time-asymmetry. The single fungible currency sits in the worst corner (maximal cost) — which is why nothing worked there.

6 · Port to the NESS substrate: the real self-repairing nets

Back on the actual decaying networks, the hedge becomes the net maintaining its own margin via free "maintenance" SGD (a decoupled repair budget), the catastrophe a decay shock (differentially lethal: high-margin nets ride it out, low-margin collapse). Reproduction is not gated on fear. Single vs decoupled maintenance, invasion assay:

Confirmed on the real agents. Same substrate, same everything — only the currency differs. Single currency repels margin-maintenance from both rare (→0.00) and common (→0.29) starts. Decoupled currency lets it invade from a 20% minority to 91% and hold at 96% when common. Mechanism check: fear-maintained nets suffered 0 health-collapse deaths vs 10 for the fearless, and decoupling made that upkeep nearly free (110 vs 69 births).

7 · What it means

A will to live is not a primitive that falls out of decay + repair + competition, and it is not about knowing you will die. It is a variance-reduction investment that competition suppresses whenever it is paid in the currency of reproduction — and it emerges the moment survival has its own budget. Put plainly:

Self-preservation evolves precisely when caring about staying alive is cheap enough to afford between the rare moments it matters — i.e. when its cost is decoupled from reproduction, and small relative to how often death actually threatens.

Controls that make this defensible: strict energy conservation asserted every tick; order-independent (synchronous) updates; cost-matched scrambled-sense control for information; frozen-type invasion assays with no mutation-bias; a neutral-drift control that caught (and retracted) an earlier false positive; and reproduction of the single-currency null on the neural substrate before the decoupled positive. Several promising early readings were refuted by harder tests along the way — the surviving claims are the ones that cleared them.
